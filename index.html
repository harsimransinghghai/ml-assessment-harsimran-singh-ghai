Meet the Makers

Your instructors

Elena Rossi

Lead Ceramicist

Trained at the Florence Academy of Ceramics, Elena brings 14 years of wheel-throwing mastery. Her work is influenced by Italian functional ware and Japanese wabi-sabi philosophy.

Wheel Throwing Sculptural Forms Raku Firing

Marcus Thorne

Glaze Specialist

A second-generation potter with deep roots in Scandinavian ceramics, Marcus specializes in glaze chemistry and surface decoration, crafting some of the most unexpected fired results you've ever seen.

Glaze Chemistry Soda Firing Surface Texture
